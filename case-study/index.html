Case study · Website & lead capture

From brochure site to quote machine.

A garage door specialist with more than 25 years in the trade had the craft, the warranties and the reviews. The website just wasn’t asking anyone to get in touch. We rebuilt it around a single job: turn a search into a quote request.

Web design, build, local SEO structure

The rebuilt homepage: promise, proof and a quote form in the first screen.

01 — The briefGood at the job. Quiet online.

Installations, repairs and maintenance for homes and businesses, a quarter-century of experience, and full warranties on products and workmanship.

None of that was doing any selling. The old page-builder site read like a brochure: the services were there, but the reasons to choose this business and the way to get a price were buried. For a trade where most buyers decide in one visit — often on a phone, often with a broken door — that’s where the enquiries leak out.

The brief was simple: make it obvious what they do, why they’re the safe choice, and how to get a quote, without breaking the pages that were already live.

02 — The approachDesign around the quote.

Every section on the new homepage answers one question a buyer has before they hand over their number.

What do you do? A plain headline and the three core services up front. Can I trust you? Years in the trade, transparent pricing, warranties and a five-star rating in the first two screens. What happens next? A short process, real reviews, an FAQ that handles objections — and a quote form that never sits more than a scroll away.

Search structure came with it: a dedicated page for every door type and service, linked from the main menu, so the site can rank for the specific jobs people search for rather than just the brand name.

03 — What we builtSix decisions that matter.

  • 01Quote form above the foldName, phone, suburb, email, the service needed and optional details — six fields, in the first screen, on every device.
  • 02Eleven service pagesResidential, commercial, sectional, roller, tilt and custom doors, gates, openers, installation, repairs and maintenance — each one a page Google can rank.
  • 03Trust on every screenA trust bar for experience, transparent pricing, warranties and punctuality, sitting directly under the hero where hesitation happens.
  • 04Two ways to convertA primary quote button and a click-to-call button side by side — because some buyers want a price and some want a person.
  • 05Editable without a developerEvery section is a field the team can update in WordPress. Phone, address, forms and imagery live in one settings screen.
  • 06No broken legacy pagesThe new design was built to sit alongside the old pages without clashing, so it shipped without a risky, all-at-once migration.

04 — Honest by designNo invented proof.

Unconfirmed claims — opening hours, response-time promises, “same day” badges — were left switched off until the business confirmed them, and the page hides any row that’s empty. Reviews are the real ones from the live listing, in their published wording. Nothing was invented to fill a gap.

It’s slower than padding a page with stock testimonials. It’s also the only kind of trust that survives a buyer checking.
