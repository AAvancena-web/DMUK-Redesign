03  Research first

4,250 search terms. 62 focus pages.

Research and strategy ran June–July 2026. The task: turn CEx’s management and IT expertise into pages prospective clients could find and understand.

4,250keywords across 7 service lines
Filteredby commercial intent
Clusteredinto focus phrases
62pages, one per phrase

Two kinds of pages

National and local

National service pages explain CEx’s expertise across Germany — digitalisation, process optimisation, AI chatbot consultancy. Local SEO pages connect those services with clients searching in specific cities — project management consultancy Munich, IT consultancy Hamburg.

Step 2 · Brief to page

Five stages for every page

A page brief with client questions and evidence; German copy reworked page by page with Johannes Reusch; on-page and technical SEO; a clear opening answer; and trust signals showing CEx’s deliverables, results and working method.

09  Trust

How to read these results.

CEx reported the fourfold increase in October 2026, comparing monthly qualified enquiries with the period before our work.

Raw enquiry totals and exact baseline dates were not supplied, so this is a client-reported result. Search Console provides separate evidence of increased visibility; it does not establish how much of the enquiry change SEO caused. Positions are averages across the stated period, Lighthouse scores are lab measurements, and everything was checked on 24 October 2026.

We think the pages drove the launch jump, though we cannot prove it: a young site also gains visibility as Google indexes more of it.

10  Takeaways and next steps

What this shows for B2B consultancies.

01

Win through services and cities

When big companies already own a short brand name in search, the way in is through services and cities.

02

Make the route to contact obvious

Give visitors a clear path from finding you to contacting you: who you help, what you do for them and how to take the next step.
