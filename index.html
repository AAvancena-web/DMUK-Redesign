02  Why search matters

Before they call you, they search.

Nobody rings you first. They look you up, compare three or four names, read what other people said, and only then decide who to contact. By the time your phone rings, the shortlist has already been made.

That’s why search is the channel with the best long-term value. The visitors already want what you sell, and the work keeps paying you back long after you publish it — a good page can bring enquiries for years, while a paid ad stops the day you stop paying.

The catch is that search is now harder and wider than before. You have to show up on Google and in AI answers, and you have to be fast, clear and trusted. That’s the work we do, in the order most likely to bring you enquiries.

There’s a second reason to act now: AI tools are still new for most businesses, and few have set their site up for them. Firms that prepare early get named first when a buyer asks an AI tool for a recommendation. The same work lifts your Google rankings too — and none of it needs a complex plan. It comes from doing the basics well: a clear site, clean facts, real reviews and useful content.

In short

Buyers shortlist before they ever contact you. Search decides who makes that shortlist — on Google and now inside AI answers.

04  Google + AI

Google still matters. AI now matters too.

Google and AI assistants now answer the same buying question in two places. Google puts an AI Overview above the blue links, while ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity name a handful of businesses directly — no results page at all.

These tools name a few businesses. If yours isn’t one of them, you’re missing buyers at the moment they decide. The good news: AI search uses the same base as Google ranking — pages it can read, clear answers, matching business details, real reviews and trusted mentions. We build that base once, so you show up in both places.

It also raises the value of trust. An AI tool only names a business it can verify, so real reviews, matching details and a clear site matter more now than ever.

10  Quick wins

The problems we fix first.

Most sites that don’t bring enquiries share the same handful of faults. These are the ones we look for on day one, because fixing them moves things fastest.

  1. Pages Google cannot index, because of a wrong setting or tag.
  2. Thin service pages that give Google nothing strong to rank.
  3. Slow pages that fail Core Web Vitals on mobile.
  4. The same title and description on many pages, which confuses Google.
  5. Broken redirects left over from old changes.
  6. Pages that no internal link points to.
  7. A weak or half-finished Google Business Profile.
  8. Business details that don’t match across the site, the profile and directories.

Each one is small, but each one holds back results. Fixing them often brings the first lift — and clears the way for the content and links to pay off.

18  Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

If your question isn’t here, send it over — someone who does the work will give you a straight answer.

What does a UK SEO agency do?

It helps people find you in search, then turns those visits into enquiries — covering research, technical fixes, content, local SEO, links and AI search.

How much does an SEO agency cost in the UK?

With us, from £495 a month on a rolling agreement. Competitive national or ecommerce work costs more, and the exact figure is scoped on the first call.

How long does SEO take to bring enquiries?

Early movement can show within weeks. Stronger growth usually takes three to six months, and the hardest national terms take longer.

Can you help us show up in AI search like ChatGPT?

Yes — by strengthening the same base used for Google ranking, so AI tools can understand, verify and name your business.

Do you only do SEO, or paid ads and CRM too?

SEO is the core. Paid ads bring faster enquiries, and CRM captures and follows up every enquiry. Services are added as and when they’ll bring or convert more enquiries.

Is SEO worth it for a small business?

It’s worth it where buyers already search for what you sell and your site can convert those visitors. It’s weaker for unproven offers or urgent sales needs.

How is local SEO different from regular SEO?

Local SEO targets nearby buyers through the map pack and your Google Business Profile. Many businesses need both local and standard SEO.

Do I need new content every month?

Often, but only where it helps. A few strong pages beat many weak ones.

What do you need from us to start?

Access to your site, analytics and Search Console, your Google Business Profile if you’re local, and a conversation about your best customers and priority services.
