      11Quick wins

      The problems we fix first.

      Most sites that don’t bring enquiries share the same handful of faults. These are the ones we look for on day one, because fixing them moves things fastest.

      1. 01

        Pages Google cannot index, because of a wrong setting or tag.

      2. 02

        Thin service pages that give Google nothing strong to rank.

      3. 03

        Problems with page speed and stability on mobile.

      4. 04

        The same title and description on many pages, which confuses Google.

      5. 05

        Broken redirects left over from old changes.

      6. 06

        Pages that no internal link points to.

      7. 07

        A weak or half-finished Google Business Profile.

      8. 08

        Business details that don’t match across the site, the profile and directories.

      Each one is small, but each one holds back results. Fixing them often brings the first lift — and clears the way for the content and links to pay off.

      What does a UK SEO agency do?+

      We help people find your business in search and make it easier for them to enquire. The work covers research, technical fixes, useful pages, local search and reporting.

      How much does an SEO agency cost in the UK?+

      Starter begins at £495 a month, covering SEO and AI-search readiness basics. Growth adds deeper AI search work and digital PR. Your proposal sets out the priorities and costs agreed for your business.

      How long does SEO take to bring enquiries?+

      First enquiries typically begin in days 61–90, while SEO commonly takes 3–6 months to show results. Your website's condition, competition and the scope of work affect timing. Results and rankings are not guaranteed.

      Can you help us show up in AI search like ChatGPT?+

      Yes. Starter includes AI-search readiness basics. Growth adds deeper AI search work, helping search tools understand your business through clear pages, accurate details and trusted mentions. Being included in an AI answer is not guaranteed.

      Do you only do SEO, or paid ads and CRM too?+

      We offer SEO, paid ads and CRM. Paid ads can help you reach buyers while SEO builds; CRM helps your team organise and follow up enquiries. We agree additional services and their costs separately.

      Is SEO worth it for a small business?+

      It’s worth it where buyers already search for what you sell and your site can convert those visitors. It’s weaker for unproven offers or urgent sales needs.

      How is local SEO different from regular SEO?+

      Local SEO targets nearby buyers through Google Maps results and your Google Business Profile. Many businesses need both local and standard SEO.

      Do I need new content every month?+

      Often, but only where it helps. A few strong pages beat many weak ones.

      How do you choose a good SEO company?+

      Look for an agency that explains the work, the cost, how progress is reported and what happens if you stop. Ask what is included in the monthly budget and who you will speak to.

      What do you need from us to start?+

      Access to your site, analytics and Search Console, your Google Business Profile if you’re local, and a conversation about your best customers and priority services.

      What does the free review include?+

      Our team calls you back to discuss your website, the outcome you want and what to improve first. The review is yours to keep. If you want us to carry out the work, we agree the scope and costs before starting.

      How long do I need to commit?+

      Plans run month to month. You can stop at the end of a paid month. SEO takes steady work, so consider the likely timeframe alongside your budget.
