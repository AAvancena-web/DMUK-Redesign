Questions

The bits people always ask.

What does a UK SEO agency actually do?

We help the right people find you in search, then make sure those visits turn into enquiries. That covers research, technical fixes, content, local SEO, links and, increasingly, visibility in AI answers.

How much does SEO cost?

Starter SEO is from £495 a month on a rolling agreement. Competitive national or ecommerce work costs more, and we scope the exact figure with you on the first call — before any work starts.

How long until I see enquiries?

Early movement often shows within weeks. Stronger growth usually takes three to six months, and the hardest national terms take longer. Anyone promising a ranking by a date is guessing.

Can you get us recommended by ChatGPT and AI search?

Yes. AI tools draw on the same foundations as Google: readable pages, clear answers, consistent business details, schema, real reviews and trusted mentions. We strengthen those so AI tools can understand, verify and name you.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Every plan is month to month. The work has to keep earning its place every month.

What do you need from us to start?

Access to your site, analytics and Search Console, your Google Business Profile if you serve a local area, and a conversation about your best customers and priority services.
