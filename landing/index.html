Is the review really free?

Yes. No card, no contract, and no obligation to work with us afterwards. If we don’t think SEO is the right move for you right now, we’ll say so.

What happens after I send my details?

We look at where you show up on Google and in AI answers, check the health of your site and the competitors who win your terms, then tell you the quickest useful moves. You keep that review whether or not you hire us.

What does working together cost?

Starter SEO is from £495 a month on a rolling agreement. Growth and Market Leader plans are scoped on the first call. You see the number before any work starts.

Is SEO worth it for a small business?

Where buyers already search for what you sell, and your site can turn visits into enquiries, yes. It’s weaker for unproven offers or if you need sales this week — in which case we’d point you to paid ads first.

Early movement often shows within weeks. Steady enquiry growth usually takes three to six months, and the most competitive national terms take longer.
