01  Why UK SEO services

Search now decides who gets the enquiry.

When someone needs what you sell, they search — on Google, in ChatGPT or Gemini, in reviews — and choose from the few businesses that show up with a clear, trustworthy answer.

SEO makes sure your business is one of those few: visible at the moment of decision, and convincing enough to earn the enquiry. Done well, it becomes the most cost-effective channel a UK business owns, because the traffic is people already looking to buy, and the work keeps paying back long after it is published.

Search itself has widened. Buyers now discover businesses through the blue links, the Google map pack, AI Overviews and assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity. They all draw on the same evidence: crawlable pages, clear answers, strong entities, consistent facts, real reviews and trusted mentions.

Where most SEO falls short. The pages that matter are thin or missing. Technical issues stop search engines crawling cleanly. Content answers the wrong question. Local signals are weak. Authority is low. Each is fixable — and a good programme fixes them in the order that creates enquiries fastest.

03  What’s included

Six workstreams, one programme.

We rarely run all six at full intensity at once — we sequence them around the work most likely to create enquiries first, then expand.

01

Keyword research & strategy

A commercial map of demand — not a list of big-volume phrases, but a model of the decisions your buyers make. We group searches by intent, weigh each by how likely it is to create an enquiry, and build the high-value pages first. Longer, specific searches from buyers close to a decision often bring the earliest enquiries.

02

Technical SEO

Making sure search engines and AI systems can crawl, render, index and trust your pages: indexation, Core Web Vitals, URL structure, internal links, structured data, redirects, sitemaps and robots. It usually comes first and often brings the quickest wins — and we plan safe migrations so a redesign never wipes out rankings.

03

On-page SEO & content

Where rankings and enquiries are won together. A precise title, logical headings, internal links, schema, and copy written to help the buyer decide — what the service is, what it includes, what it costs, what risks to avoid and what happens after they enquire. Around it, guides and comparison pages.

04

Local SEO

Often the fastest route to enquiries for anyone serving an area. We improve the Google Business Profile, keep name, address and phone consistent everywhere, earn and reply to reviews, build citations and create genuinely useful location pages — so the site and profile tell the same story.

05

Link building & digital PR

Authority is the lever for harder, higher-value terms. We earn relevant mentions and links from real publications, industry sites and local organisations through useful content, data, commentary and outreach — links a human editor would choose to give, which hold their value as algorithms change.

06

AI search optimisation (GEO)

Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity recommend businesses drawn from crawlable information, clear answers, strong entities, schema, consistent facts, real reviews and trusted mentions. We make your business easy for an AI system to understand and quote.

Ecommerce & international

Category structure, product copy, faceted-navigation control and large-catalogue crawl issues; language and region targeting for international markets. Added only when they will create enquiries or sales.

Content depth & topical authority

A connected cluster that answers every question a buyer asks — what it is, how it compares, what it costs, what could go wrong — around one clear main page, with real expertise signals.

Turning rankings into enquiries

A clear call to action, a visible phone number, a short form and the proof a buyer needs on every important page. A page that ranks well and converts poorly is only half the job.

06  Timing

How long SEO takes to work.

SEO is an investment that compounds, so it helps to know what to expect and when.

In the first few weeks, technical fixes and local improvements often produce early movement: invisible pages get indexed, the Google Business Profile works harder, and quick-win terms begin to climb — frequently bringing the first new enquiries.

Over three to six months, content and authority take hold and the site starts ranking for the mid-difficulty terms that bring steady enquiries. Competitive national terms take longer, so we win the achievable terms first and approach the hardest ones from strength.

10  Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

Straight answers for owners and marketing teams weighing up scope, cost, timing, local search and AI visibility before a call.

What do UK SEO services actually include?

Good SEO combines six workstreams: research and strategy to find the searches that bring buyers, technical work so Google and AI systems can crawl and understand the site, on-page work and content, local SEO to win the map pack, link building and digital PR to earn authority, and AI search optimisation. The mix is set by what will create enquiries fastest for your market, then reported every month.

How much do SEO services cost in the UK?

Digital Movement UK’s SEO services start at £495 a month for Starter SEO, on a rolling monthly agreement with no lock-in. Price rises with competition, the number of pages that need building or fixing, and how much technical debt the site carries. The exact figure is scoped on the first call, and the free review tells you the likely budget before you commit.

How long do SEO services take to work in the UK?

Early movement often appears within a few weeks once technical fixes and local improvements land. Stronger enquiry growth usually builds over three to six months, and competitive national terms can take longer. The monthly report shows progress long before the headline rankings move.

What is the difference between SEO and local SEO?

Standard SEO wins listings across the whole country or a sector. Local SEO wins buyers searching near you, where Google shows a map pack of three businesses above the normal results. Many businesses need both: local SEO for nearby demand and organic SEO for the wider market.

Do UK SEO services help with AI search like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews?

Yes. AI search visibility grows from the same foundations as organic ranking: crawlable pages, clear answers near the top, strong entities, schema, consistent business facts, real reviews and trusted mentions. We strengthen those signals so AI tools can understand your business and cite it.

What is technical SEO and why does it matter?

Technical work makes sure search engines and AI systems can crawl, render, index and trust your pages — crawl and indexation, speed and Core Web Vitals, URL structure, internal links, structured data, redirects, sitemaps and mobile health. The best content cannot rank if Google cannot reach it cleanly.

How do you measure SEO results?

We track rankings, organic traffic, conversions and the number of qualified enquiries, and tie each enquiry back to the page and search that produced it. AI answer visibility is tracked alongside Google, because both now shape how buyers discover a business.

Are SEO services worth it for a small UK business?

It is worth it when buyers already search for what you sell and the website can convert those visitors into enquiries. It is a poor fit when the offer is unproven or you need sales this week. We recommend the smallest useful first phase, and say plainly if paid ads or local work would be faster.

How do you choose a good SEO company?

A good SEO company explains the work before asking for trust: which pages it will build, which technical issues it will fix, how it earns authority, how progress is reported and how the work turns into enquiries. Look for clear reporting, a rolling agreement with no long lock-in, and a focus on enquiries over award claims.
